01 — BRAND IDEA

Speech is intimate infrastructure. Homan keeps it at home.

Homan (home + human) is the next chapter of Muesli: the same promise — your words never leave your machine — carried by a mark that stands on its own. Everything the brand does should feel calm, personal, and quietly technical.

LOCAL BY DEFAULT

Everything runs on-device. The brand never borrows cloud metaphors — no arrows to servers, no sync glyphs.

HUMAN, NOT BOT

No guest joins the meeting. Voice is portrayed as conversation between people, never as a robot listening in.

QUIET CONFIDENCE

Understated surfaces, one warm accent, plain language. The product speaks softly because it has nothing to hide.

02 — THE MARK

Two waveforms, one letter

The mark is a capital H built from two waveforms. Each stem is its own two-bar wave — one voice on each side of the conversation. A solid crossbar joins them: the home where the two waves meet.

ALeft wave — the first voice: two bars, rising amplitude.
BRight wave — the second voice, mirrored in reply.
CCrossbar — the home; a solid beam holding the two waves together.
